Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

At Spendly, we are committed to protecting your personal information and your financial data. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking services of Spendly. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or financial information to third parties.

We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting) to run our service, but these providers have no access to your raw personal data without our authorization.
